Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Who we are

TsofenOr — צופן אור, at tsofenor.com, is a Project Elijah venture operated by Ronen Priewer in Israel. For any question or request about privacy, write to privacy@projectelijah.io.

What information we collect

We receive only what you choose to send us through the forms on the site:

  • Signing up for research updates: your email address and, optionally, your name and a message, so that we can tell you about new findings and get back to you.

We use these details only to get back to you and for the purpose of the form you filled in. We do not sell them, rent them out or use them for advertising.

To understand how many people visit the site and which pages they read, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It stores no cookies, does not fingerprint your device and does not track you across sites. We see totals only: page views, country, the site you came from and the type of browser.

Our hosting provider, Netlify, processes technical information such as your IP address in order to serve the pages and protect the site from abuse.

Cookies

We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising.

Who handles the information

Netlify (site hosting and form submissions) and Cloudflare (visit statistics). Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address for that purpose. We do not pass information to anyone else, unless the law requires it.

How long it is kept

Form submissions are kept only as long as they are needed to get back to you or to keep you updated about the venture. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your rights

Under Israel’s Protection of Privacy Law, 5741–1981, and where relevant under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you may ask to see the information we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it. Write to privacy@projectelijah.io and we will reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
